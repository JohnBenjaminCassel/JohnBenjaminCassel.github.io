builds systems for stewarding landscapes and infrastructure by multiple criteria


I'm a discovery-driven domain designer and software engineer interested in multifunctional agriculture/landscape/infrastructure design possibilities from a variety of professional and personal perspectives, including:

  • Design and planning: investigating design questions of understanding, intervening, and evaluating landscape and infrastructure configurations over time to promote long-term flourishing
  • Enjoyment, appeal, and attention: sharing the delight and importance of attending to and caring for the biological, ecological, and infrastructural world we are a part of
  • Intervention program operations: changing the shape of the criteria being explored by undertaking programs pursuing broader objectives
  • Research support: providing data models, along with operational methods, that effectively support investigations into landscape and infrastructure performance
  • Knowledge representation: capturing understanding of current and potential organisms, ecosystems, and related stewardship infrastracture
  • Intervention Tooling: improving our ability to act appropriately at scale by improving the sensing, decision, and actuation performance of our tools
  • Personal cultivation: enjoying more multi-functional spaces by introducing plants and tending to them
For me, our journey to these ends is as important as the destination, so I'm also passionate about building great design and engineering cultures wherever I work, aiming to be open, engaged, present, communicative, courageous, and kind.

In previous academic work, I explored discovery and planning processes in risk governance, engineering design, and strategic foresight. I'm still interested in these topics.

Outside of these interests, I enjoy reading, listening to music, podcasts, jogging, keeping up with friends, and just generally being here to enjoy life.

I can be reached on LinkedIn, Goodreads, Facebook, by email, and via other channels as appropriate.
